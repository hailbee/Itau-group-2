so in my original code i have fraud and real text and a label saying 1 if actual spoof attempt or 0 if not. i turned these into image encodings using siglip. i then used a siamese mlp model and contrastive pairwise loss to get "better" embeddings that are "spoof aware" because spoofs are closer, non-spoofs are farther. now the next goal is to turn the fraud and real text into the siglip text embeddings and then learn a space where the text embeddings are mapped to image embeddings because although image embeddings have more rich information, they are also more expensive to compute so essentially i want to "translate" the information.

Track two things during experiments:
Alignment: mean cosine(student(s), teacher(s))
Spoof pair ROC-AUC: cosine(student(fraud), student(real)) vs label
If alignment improves but ROC-AUC drops, it usually means either: the student is matching teacher globally but distorting pair geometry relevant to your task, or your teacher spoof-aware space is doing something that your text embedding can’t fully predict At that point you’d consider (optional) geometry-preserving distillation — but you don’t need to jump there yet.
